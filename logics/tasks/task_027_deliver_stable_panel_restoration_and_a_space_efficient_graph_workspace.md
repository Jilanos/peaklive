  Width policy (request AC8): `WorkspaceHeaderBar` (`src/peaklive/ui/panels/workspace_header.py`) now takes `required`/`deferrable` registrations via `add(widget, deferrable=...)`. Required controls (fit, Start/Stop, both cursor-placement buttons, the A/B/delta readout, the empty-state note) always stay directly on the row. Deferrable controls (measurement-visibility toggle, Follow live, fit-Y, the workspace-mode selector — in that fold order, so the mode selector is the last to fold) move into a `QMenu` (via one `QWidgetAction` hosting a small panel, so the very same live widgets — not copies — travel between the row and the menu) once `_available_width()` says they no longer fit. `_available_width()` deliberately does not read `self.width()`: since this bar has no stretch of its own in its host title row, its own width is just an echo of whatever it currently shows, so once anything is deferred the row shrinks to match and the same comparison would find "still not enough room" forever, even after the window grows back.
